## English (colar na Apple)
1. SCREEN RECORDING: recorded on a physical iPhone, starting from launching the app and showing the typical flow (menu, choose driver, choose city, race, rematch, online room). [anexar/link do vídeo]
2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE: Race Cities is a free 3D kart racing game for the whole family (rated 9+ for cartoon fantasy violence: banana, rocket items). Players race animal drivers through stylized cities of the world (Rio de Janeiro, Paris, Tokyo, Beijing, etc.). It is entertainment only. No chat, no user-generated content.
3. HOW TO USE: no account, no login, no sign-up and no purchases. No ads in this version. Open the app, tap Play, choose a driver and a city, race. The game works fully offline (all files are inside the app package). To test online mode: tap "Online com Amigos" > "Criar sala" on one device and enter the 4-letter code on another device (optional; a single device is enough to review).
4. EXTERNAL SERVICES: only for the optional online mode: PeerJS public signaling server (peerjs.com) and Google public STUN servers (stun.l.google.com), used to connect two devices directly (WebRTC). Only a temporary room code and a nickname are exchanged; nothing is stored. Open-source libraries bundled in the app: three.js (3D) and PeerJS, plus Google Fonts files (Baloo 2, Lilita One) stored inside the app. No analytics, no ad SDK, no login/payment provider, no AI service.
5. REGIONS: available only in Brazil (Portuguese). Features and content are identical everywhere.
6. REGULATION / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL: not a regulated industry. Music was created by us with Suno on a paid Pro plan that includes commercial use rights (created and downloaded on 14/11/2026, original files kept). All city, monument and store names are fictional; no third-party brands. Proof of the Suno plan available on request.

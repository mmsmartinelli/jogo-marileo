## English (United States)
**Name:** Race Cities

**Short description (max 80):**
3D kart racing around the world's cities. Play with friends, online or offline!
(79 characters)

**Full description:**
Race with Mari, Leo and six more animal drivers in Race Cities, a colorful 3D kart racing game through cities around the world, made for the whole family to play together!

🌍 AROUND THE WORLD
Osasco, Rio de Janeiro, New York, London, Paris, Rome, Cairo, Dubai, Agra, Beijing, Tokyo and Sydney! Every city has its own flavor: stylized landmarks, busy streets, changing weather and times of day.

♾️ ENDLESS CITIES
Surprise City and the Endless Cup create brand-new races every time: a different track, by day, sunset or night, with rain, sand or falling petals.

🚕 THE CITY IS ALIVE
Taxis, double-decker buses, camels, tuk-tuks, kangaroos crossing the road, fire hydrants spraying water and flocks of pigeons taking off as you pass.

🎁 ITEM BOXES
Turbo, shield, invincibility star, rocket, banana, oil, squid ink and fake box. Use them at the right time to pass your friends!

👫 PLAY WITH FRIENDS
• Online with friends: create a room, share the code and race together, up to 8 players, each on their own phone.
• Up to 4 players on the same screen with controllers.
• Rematch without leaving the room.

🏆 GAME MODES
• Grand Prix with 4-city cups and a podium
• Quick Race
• Time Trial with saved records
• Challenges with stars in every city

🎮 EASY TO PLAY
Simple on-screen controls, auto-accelerate option, drifting with mini-turbo, ramps with tricks and three difficulty levels.

✅ No purchases and no sign-up. Works offline (only online mode needs a connection). Contains ads once enabled.
